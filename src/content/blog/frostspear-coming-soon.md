FrostSpear: Tundra Hunt is almost here. It's an Ice Age adventure where you tap to throw your
spear at dire wolves, sabertooths, woolly rhinos and mammoths across 200 levels, from the
Frozen Valley to the Frost Kingdom.

## Made for kids first

The first levels are gentle on purpose. A few slow wolves, friendly hints and plenty of spare
spears let younger players learn without frustration. New beasts arrive a few levels apart, each
with a habit to learn, and every tenth level ends in a boss fight that gives older players a real
challenge.

## No ads, nothing to buy

FrostSpear has no ads and no in-app purchases. Spears and hoods are unlocked by playing. The game
collects no personal information and works fully offline. The details are in its
[privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Play it before launch

We're starting a closed test on Google Play. If you'd like to play before launch, email
[contact@playflex.net](mailto:contact@playflex.net?subject=FrostSpear%20closed%20test) with the
address you use for Google Play.
