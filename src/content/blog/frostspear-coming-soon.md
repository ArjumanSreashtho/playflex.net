FrostSpear: Tundra Hunt is almost here. It's an Ice Age adventure where you tap to throw your
spear at wolves, sabre-tooths, woolly rhinos and mammoths across 200 levels of frozen tundra,
caves and starry nights.

## Made for kids first

The first chapter is gentle on purpose. A few slow wolves, friendly hints and plenty of spare
spears let younger players learn the game without frustration. New beasts arrive a few levels
apart, so there's always something new to meet, and boss beasts every ten levels give older
players a real challenge.

## No ads, no purchases

FrostSpear has no ads and no in-app purchases, collects no personal information and works fully
offline. You can read the details in its [privacy policy](/frostspear/privacy/).

## Want to play early?

We're starting a closed test on Google Play. If you'd like to try it before launch, write to
[contact@playflex.net](mailto:contact@playflex.net).
