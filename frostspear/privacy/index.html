FrostSpear: Tundra Hunt Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

The short version

  • FrostSpear: Tundra Hunt is a single-player game made for children, teens and families.
  • We do not collect, share or sell personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Game progress is saved only on the device. The game works fully offline.

1. Who we are

FrostSpear: Tundra Hunt (“the game”, Android package com.playflex.frostspear) is made by PlayFlex, an independent game developer in Bangladesh (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when the game is played. Questions are welcome at contact@playflex.net.

2. Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), voice or any other personal information. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and we have no server that receives data from the game.

3. Information saved on the device

So that a player can continue where they left off, the game saves in its private storage on the device: levels completed, stars, meat and XP, unlocked spears and hoods, trophy counts, daily hunt progress, and sound, music and vibration settings.

This data never leaves the device through the game. If device backup is turned on, Android may include it in the device backup to the player’s Google Account so progress can be restored on a new phone; that is controlled by the Google Account settings, and we cannot access those backups.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: a short buzz when a beast is hit. It can be turned off in the game’s settings.
  • Internet: declared by the app framework (Capacitor) that runs the game. The game itself makes no network connections, and its security policy blocks connections to any website or server.

The game does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Links, ads and purchases

The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no links that lead out of the game.

6. Children

The game is designed for children and families and is offered under the Google Play Families policy. Because it collects no personal information at all, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in their country). Parents and guardians can write to us at any time with questions.

7. Deleting data

Uninstalling the game deletes all of its data from the device. It can also be cleared at any time in Android Settings › Apps › FrostSpear › Storage › Clear storage.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and its date before the change is released, and update the game’s Data safety information on Google Play. We will not start collecting personal information without updating this policy first.
